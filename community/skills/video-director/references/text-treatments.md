# Text treatments

Choose how words appear and move. Pick per film and record the choice in the storyboard's `style` field. Sizes are font size as a share of frame height; times are in seconds.

## How lines arrive

| Method | Timing | Used for | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Word by word, fade | One steady pace for the film, about 0.1-0.3 s between words, or each word with the voice or beat; each fade about 0.4 s | Hooks, questions, end lines | Apple "Designed by Apple in California"; Superhuman launch; Google "How big is space" short; Patagonia (0.17 s, with and without a voice) |
| Word by word, cut on | 0.2-0.8 s per word or word group | Energetic headlines; an end line built on the last live shot | OpenAI "Sites"; Apple "Privacy on iPhone" end line |
| Words rise from a mask | 0.3 s per word, 0.1-0.3 s apart | Titles | Vox explainers |
| Whole line, fade with a small rise | 0.4-0.5 s, rising about 1% of frame | Spec lists, chapter titles, definitions | iPhone Air spec lines |
| Whole line, cut on | instant | Captions, labels, claims over footage | most footage-led films |
| Typed | see "Typed product text" in `text-and-captions.md` | Product input; labels with a product feel | Google "Loretta"; Linear Agent |

Use whole-line setting unless word arrivals serve the phrase. If words arrive one by one, use one cadence for the whole film: either the words sweep in at a steady quick pace (about 0.1-0.3 s apart, so a short line is in within about half a second), or each word lands with the voice, lyric or beat. A pause before the key word can be up to about 0.9 s (Airbnb "We Accept"). Never pause inside a phrase where the voice or beat doesn't.

Lines hold 1-3 s; up to 4-8 s when a line labels a long action. Lines leave on the cut, with a camera move, by fading, by erasing letters, or by being replaced in place.

## Treatments

| Treatment | When | Movement | Size and position | Across cuts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic type as the whole picture | Manifestos, openers, definitions with no footage | Calm: words fade 0.3 s apart, hold 1-3 s, leave by fade or with the camera. Energetic: letters fly in, stretch, burst; moves 0.1-0.6 s, hold 0.5-2 s | Calm 2.5-4%, centred; energetic 3-30%+ | Words shrink into the next frame, or the same sentence returns at the end |
| Type locked to an object (label chips) | Naming parts of a diagram | A thin line draws (0.1 s), a box grows from it (0.1 s), the word shows (0.1-0.2 s); a new word swaps in place by cut | 1.5-4%, beside or on the object | Stays put while the object or background changes |
| Type on a curve or split around a product | Lines that belong to a character or a product | Letters write on along the curve about 20 per second; hold about 2 s; erase at the same speed | 4-6%, above, around or either side of the subject | Belongs to one shot |
| Label with a leader line | Naming a physical effect | Line draws out from the object, word revealed left to right over about 1 s | About 2.5% | Moves with the object |
| Side label beside a product shot | Comparisons, step names over one locked camera | Fade in 0.2-0.5 s, hold 4-8 s, change in place | 3-4% per line, two lines, beside the product | Same spot every time, so it reads as one system |
| Spec or chapter card on a plain ground | Hardware spec lists, tutorial chapter titles | Fade with a small rise 0.4-0.5 s, hold 1.5-2.5 s, fade or cut | 3-5%, centred | Alternates with product shots |
| Line held over fast cuts | A closing promise or title over a montage | Words reveal about 0.25 s each, then the line holds still | 3-10%, centred or upper third | Held still while 3-5 shots cut underneath |
| Captions of speech | Talking heads, narrated films watched muted | 2-6 word chunks cut on every 0.5-2 s; the spoken word may light up | 1.8-4%, bottom or about two thirds down, in a box | Run across cuts and inserts |
| Lower third | A speaker's name and role | Bar or slide build 0.3-0.9 s, hold 3-5 s, wipe or fade out | Name about 4%, role about 2%, bottom left or right | Carries across cuts within one speaker |
| Typed product text | Showing a request to the product | Letter by letter or 1-3 character chunks; cursor when the field is the subject | Real UI size, or cropped huge at 7-10% | The prompt stays as the camera pulls back, becomes the result's heading, or is replaced by a status line |
| Giant single word or letter | One payoff word; showing a typeface | Cut on and hold 1-2 s, or scale up and break apart in 0.5 s | 12-80% | Ends the scene, or shrinks into the next frame |
| Giant number or counter | One "how many" or speed claim | Counts up about 30 per second for under a second, or swaps once a second | 6-40% | Holds while a device or the camera moves past |
| Word swap keeping shared words | An end line that turns into the brand line | Unshared words fade, new words fade in, shared words do not move; about 0.7 s | 2.5-5%, centred on the last shot | Single shot |
| Text that becomes an object | A phrase that turns into the next scene or the logo | Scales to fill the frame in 0.3 s, or a cursor dot grows into a shape | Grows from line size to full frame | The text is the transition |
| Text as a real object in the scene | Live-action explainers, comedy stats | Hand-lettered cards, a clapperboard, a sign the camera passes | 10-20% when close | Persists in wide shots |
| End line on the film's last shot | The close | Fades or builds word by word on the last live shot or ground, holds 2-3 s, then the logo | 2.5-4%, centred at mid-height | The last surface carries the line and the logo |
| No added text | Hardware teasers, product-only explainers, showreels | n/a | n/a | n/a |

## Emphasis

Pick one marking for the film:
- **Brightness, or a tint that settles.** The key word starts a lighter tint and settles to the line colour over about 1 s. Or the rest of the line is dimmer than the key word.
- **Italic of the same face**, at the same size.
- **A colour matched to what the word labels.** For example, the feature's own halo colour, or green for done and red for failed.
- **The house colour** on a highlight bar or underline.
- **A drawn mark** over a letter or a circle around a fact, drawn on in about 0.5 s.
- **None.** This is common in brand and footage films.
